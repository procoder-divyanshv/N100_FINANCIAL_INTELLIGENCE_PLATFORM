# Nifty 100 Financial Analytics & Valuation System

A comprehensive multi-page Streamlit dashboard built for analyzing the fundamental financial health, sector distributions, and peer benchmarks of the Nifty 100 universe.

## Sprint 4 Features
* **01 Home:** Macro-level KPI summaries, debt-free counters, and interactive sector breakdown charts.
* **02 Profile:** Deep dive into individual company financials, 10-year P&L trends, ROCE trajectories, and summarized pros/cons.
* **03 Screener:** Multi-factor stock screening with preset strategies (Quality, Value, Growth) and direct CSV export capabilities.
* **04 Peers:** Radar chart benchmarking and side-by-side KPI comparison across 11 specific industry groups.
* **05 Trends:** Interactive YoY metric overlay analysis for up to 3 financial variables simultaneously.
* **06 Sectors:** Bubble chart risk/reward visualizations (ROE vs. Book Value mapped to Market Cap).
* **07 Capital:** Treemap visualizing Nifty 100 company distribution across broad sectors.
* **08 Reports:** Centralized access hub for historical BSE annual report PDF filings.
